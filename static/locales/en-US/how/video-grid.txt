Create a video from the camera

The @Camera stream is a great way to get a grid of colors from the world. 
What if we want to see those colors, recreating the image the @Camera sees?

An @Image takes a grid of colors and draws it as one picture, so we can hand it the @Camera's colors directly.

\
width: 24
height: 24
colors: Camera(width × 1px height × 1px 33ms)
Stage([Image(colors "what the camera sees" 12m)])
\

Every @Image needs a description, because a grid of colors has no words of its own for someone who can't see it.

We can draw the picture with letters instead of squares by giving it a /glyph/, a function from one color to the text that stands for it.

\
width: 24
height: 24
colors: Camera(width × 1px height × 1px 33ms)
Stage([Image(colors "the camera, drawn with dark and light circles" 12m glyph: ƒ(color•Color) (color.lightness < 50%) ? "⬤" "◯")])
\
