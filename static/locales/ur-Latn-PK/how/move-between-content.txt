Teer wali kunjiyon se content ke darmiyan chalein

Kabhi kabhi aap chahte hain ke dekhne wale content ko ek ek hissa karke dekhein, jaise slide show mein, aage / aur / peechhe teer wali kunjiyon se.

Is tareeqe ka har rup inhi teen hisson se banta hai:

• Us content ki ek @List jo aap dikhana chahte hain
• Us content ki ek @Key dhaara, jo har baar kunji dabane par badalti hai
• Us content ki ek @Reaction jo yaad rakhta hai ke abhi aap content ke kis hisse par hain

Yeh iska sabse chhota rup hai. Pehle stage par click karein taake woh sunne lage, phir baayein aur daayein teer ki kunji dabayein:

\
slides3: ['pehla' 'doosra' 'teesra']

kunji2: Kunji()

index2•#: 1 … ∆ kunji2 …
	(kunji2 = 'ArrowRight') ? ((index2 < slides3.lambaai()) ? index2 + 1 index2)
		(kunji2 = 'ArrowLeft') ? ((index2 > 1) ? index2 - 1 index2)
			index2

Jumla(slides3[index2])
\

 Yeh @Reaction is ka dil hai. Yeh \1\ se shuru hota hai, aur jab bhi @Key badalti hai, yeh tay karta hai ke naya number kya hona chahiye.
Agar daayein teer ki kunji dabi, to yeh ek barhata hai; agar baayein teer ki kunji dabi, to ek ghatata hai; warna number wahi rehta hai.

Yeh izaafi \?\ jaanch number ko list ke siron se aage jaane se rokti hain.
Inke baghair aap aakhri sire se aage nikal jaate aur dikhane ke liye koi slide na hoti.
Agar aap chahte hain ke aakhri slide ke baad dobara pehli slide aa jaye, to aap woh bhi kar sakte hain.

Note: teer wali kunjiyon ke naam yeh hain \'ArrowLeft'\, \'ArrowRight'\, \'ArrowUp'\, aur \'ArrowDown'\ na ho.
Aap sirf yeh bhi likh sakte hain \'Left'\ aur \'Right'\, aur kunji ke naam aapki apni zubaan mein bhi kaam karte hain.

Dekhein ke \index\ sirf ek number hai, is liye yeh / koi bhi / qism ke content ke liye kaam karta hai, sirf matn ke liye nahin.
Is @List ko badal kar ek list rakhein @Group ya poore scene, aur baaqi pattern bilkul nahin badalta:

\
slides3: [
	Jumla('teen hisson ki kahani' size: 3m)
	Group(Tah() [Jumla('🐈') Jumla('ek billi nazar aati hai')])
	Jumla('khatam 🎉')
]

kunji2: Kunji()

index2•#: 1 … ∆ kunji2 …
	(kunji2 = 'ArrowRight') ? ((index2 < slides3.lambaai()) ? index2 + 1 index2)
		(kunji2 = 'ArrowLeft') ? ((index2 > 1) ? index2 - 1 index2)
			index2

slides3[index2]
\

 Ek @Scene bhi yeh kar sakta hai, aur kam ginti ke saath.
Isay @Scene/pause dein taake yeh khud kabhi na hile, aur @Scene/step taake yeh ek tukre ko kisi bhi taraf hila sake:

\
kunji2: Kunji()
Manzar([
		Jumla('teen hisson ki kahani' size: 3m)
		Group(Tah() [Jumla('🐈') Jumla('ek billi nazar aati hai')])
		Jumla('khatam 🎉')
	] waqfa qadam: ∆ kunji2 ? ((kunji2 = 'ArrowLeft') ? -1 (kunji2 = 'ArrowRight') ? 1 ø) ø)
\

 Yahan likhne ke liye koi \?\ jaanch nahin hai, kyunke @Scene apne siron par khud ruk jata hai, aur kisi ko yaad rakhne ki zaroorat nahin ke kaun sa tukra nazar aa raha hai.
Poochhein @Spotlight agar aap kehna chahte hain / 10 mein se 3 / stage par.

Note: poore mansooba mein yeh numaish gah mein dekhein: < slide show @https://wordplay.dev/project/example-SlideShow?play>

 Yeh @Reaction upar wala tareeqa ab bhi sahi hai jab aap jis cheez par se guzar rahe hain woh output nahin hota — alfaaz, rang ya number ki list, ya koi aur cheez jis se aap tukra banate hain.
