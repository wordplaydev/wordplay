Kisi harf ko hilne ke qabil banayein

Kabhi kabhi yeh madadgaar hota hai ke aap @Phrase ko keyboard, mouse, ya ungli se hilne ke qabil banayein.

Yeh karne ke liye, hum mila sakte hain @Phrase ke saath @Placement deta hai:

\
Stage(
	[Jumla('mujhe hilaain' jagah: Thikana())]
	jagah: Jagah(0m 0m -10m)
)
\

 Keyboard ke teer istemaal karna ya is par click karna @Stage ko tabdeel karega @Placement, jis se @Phrase us simt mein hilega.

Kya aap jaante hain ke @Placement ko is ke saath istemaal kiya ja sakta hai / kisi bhi cheez / ke saath jo leti hai @Place? 
Misaal ke taur par @Stage mein ek @Place hota hai jo tay karta hai ke is ka camera kahan dekh raha hai.
Aayein aazmayein ke @Placement ke liye @Stage ki jagah tay karein, aur dekhein kya hota hai.

\
Stage(
  [
	Jumla('🐈' jagah: Jagah(-2m 2m))
	Jumla('🐈' jagah: Jagah(2m -2m))
	Jumla('🐈' jagah: Jagah(-1m 3m))
  ]
  jagah: Thikana(Jagah(0m 0m -10m))
)
\

 Is se camera hilta hai! (Dhyaan dein: hum aaghaz karte hain @Placement ke saath ek \z\ qeemat se \10m\ taake camera har cheez se peeche rahe.)
