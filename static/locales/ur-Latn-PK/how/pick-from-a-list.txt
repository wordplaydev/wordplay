Fehrist se koi bhi cheez ittefaq se chunein

Kya aap chahte hain ke aap ki mansooba har baar chalne par aap ko hairaan kare? Intikhabaat ki ek fehrist banayein aur us mein se ek chunein @Random na ho.

@Random hamein ek ittefaqi number de sakta hai, aur fehrist mein number se cheez dhoondi ja sakti hai \[n]\ na ho.
In dono ko mila kar ek ittefaqi emoji chunein:

\
emoji: ['🌈' '🌟' '🎈' '🦄' '🍩']
Jumla(emoji.ittefaqi())
\

 Aap yeh bhi istemaal kar sakte hain @Random do numbers ke saath, jo un ke darmiyan ka ek poora number chunta hai.
Misaal ke taur par, har second / naya / ittefaqi emoji chunne ke liye, yeh milayein @Time aur ek @Reaction deta hai:

\
emoji: ['🌈' '🌟' '🎈' '🦄' '🍩']
chunein: emoji[1] … ∆ Waqt(1000ms) … emoji[Ittefaqi(1 emoji.lambaai())]
Jumla(chunein)
\

 Har tick par @Time, yeh @Reaction ek naya hairaan kun nateeja chunta hai.
