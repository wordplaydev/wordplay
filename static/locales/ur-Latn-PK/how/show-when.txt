Qeemat ke mutabiq mukhtalif cheezein dikhayein

Agar aap chahte hain ke @Phrase kisi cheez ke mutabiq badal jaye, jaise score ya kisi kunji ka dabna, to kya karein?

Hum shartiya \?\ istemaal kar ke do cheezon mein se ek chun sakte hain.
Pehle shart likhein, phir \?\, phir woh qeemat jo shart sahi hone par istemaal ho, phir woh qeemat jo shart ghalat hone par istemaal ho.

Yeh ek khush chehra hai jo har baar space bar dabane par udaas ho jata hai, aur phir dobara khush ho jata hai:

\
khush: ⊤ … ∆ Kunji(' ') … ~khush
Jumla(khush ? '😊' '😢')
\

 Yeh @Reaction shuru mein \happy\ ke tor par \⊤\ (sahi) hota hai, aur isay \~\ se palat deta hai jab bhi space dabaya jaye.
Phir shartiya \?\ sahi chehra chun leta hai.

Aap shartiya izhaar ko jod kar do se zyada cheezon mein se chun sakte hain:

\
score: 0 … ∆ Kunji('a') … score + 1
Jumla(score < 3 ? '🥚' score < 6 ? '🐣' '🐥')
\

 Kuch baar \a\ dabayein aur dekhein ke anda phoot kar chooza ban jata hai!
