Kai zubaanon ke dekhne walon ko salaam karein

Ek @Phrase har dekhne wale ko uski chuni hui zubaanon ke mutabiq theek tarjuma dikhata hai. Agar aapke dekhne wale Spanish bolte hain, to aap unse Spanish mein baat kar sakte hain; agar woh Spanish aur English dono bolte hain — Spanglish! — to aap dono milaa sakte hain.

Ek @TextLiteral ko kai tarjume dein, jin mein ek kai zubaanon wala bhi ho, aur @wordplay har dekhne wale ke liye sab se munasib tarjuma chun leta hai:

\
Jumla("hola"/es"hello"/en"hola gentleman"/es_en)
\

 Jis dekhne wale ki pasandeeda zubaan sirf Spanish hai, woh yeh dekhta hai: / hola /. Jo sirf English pasand karta hai, woh yeh dekhta hai: / hello /. Jo Spanish aur English dono pasand karta hai, woh yeh dekhta hai: / hola gentleman / — yaani Spanglish wala tarjuma.

Wordplay sab se khaas mel ko tarjeeh deta hai, is liye jab dono lagu ho sakte hon to ek zubaan wala tarjuma hamesha kai zubaanon wale tarjume par ghalib aata hai.

Spanglish wala tarjuma poora Spanish mein parha jaata hai, yaani apni pehli zubaan mein. Har lafz ko uski apni zubaan mein parhwane ke liye, hissaon par alag alag tag lagayein aur unhein jodein:

\
Jumla("hola "/es + "gentleman"/en)
\
