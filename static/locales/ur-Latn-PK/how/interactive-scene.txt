Scenes ki ek interactive silsila banayein

Kabhi kabhi hum aisi kahaaniyan sunana chahte hain jo agle hisse par jaane se pehle dekhne wale ke kuch karne ka intezaar karti hain.

Iske liye hum istemaal kar sakte hain @Scene, lekin rakhein @Boolean qeematein us mawaad ke beech jo hum dikhana chahte hain. Misaal ke taur par, yahan ek click har agle hisse par le jaaye ga, @Button deta hai:

\
Manzar([
		Jumla('mujh par click karein')
		Button()
		Jumla('mujh par dobara click karein' muddat: 1s)
		Button()
		Jumla('nahin, waqai, mujh par poori taaqat se click karein!!' muddat: 1s)
		Button()
		Jumla('kya aap ne abhi tak mujh par click kiya?' muddat: 1s)
	])
\

 Yeh @Scene pehla dikhata hai @Phrase, phir intezaar karta hai @Button ke sach hone ka, aur jab woh sach ho jaaye, to agle par chala jaata hai @Phrase na ho.

Aage badhne ke liye aap kuch bhi istemaal kar sakte hain, bas woh sach ya jhoot hona chahiye. 
Misaal ke taur par, hum apni aawaaz istemaal kar sakte hain! 
Agar hum kaafi zor se chillayein, to scene aage badh jaaye gi:

\
Manzar([
		Jumla('kya wahan koi hai?')
		AwaazKiUnchaai() > .5
		Jumla('mujhe aap ki aawaaz nahin aa rahi, dobara kahein?' muddat: 1s)
		AwaazKiUnchaai() > .7
		Jumla('zara zor se kahein...' muddat: 1s)
		AwaazKiUnchaai() > .9
		Jumla('oh, salaam!' muddat: 1s)
	])
\

 Jab har @Phrase ko ek hi cheez ka intezaar karna ho, @Scene/until ise ek hi baar kehta hai, har ek ke beech likhne ke bajaye. Yeh wahi karta hai jo upar ki pehli misaal karti hai:

\
Manzar([
		Jumla('mujh par click karein')
		Jumla('mujh par dobara click karein' muddat: 1s)
		Jumla('nahin, waqai, mujh par poori taaqat se click karein!!' muddat: 1s)
		Jumla('kya aap ne abhi tak mujh par click kiya?' muddat: 1s)
	] jabTak: Button())
\

 Ek likhein @Boolean list mein jab intezaar hon / alag alag /, jaise chillane wali misaal mein, aur istemaal karein @Scene/until jab woh sab ek jaise hon.
Aap dono istemaal kar sakte hain: ek @Boolean list mein apna alag intezaar hai, us ek ke ilawa jo @Scene/until maangta hai.

Note: @Scene/until intezaar karta hai / ke baad / har @Phrase ka apna @Phrase/duration, is ki jagah nahin.

Agar aap chahte hain ke @Scene khud se kabhi na hile, aur sirf wahin jaye jahan aap bhejein, to istemaal karein @Scene/pause ke saath @Scene/step deta hai:

\
kunji2: Kunji()
Manzar([
		Jumla('ek')
		Jumla('do')
		Jumla('teen')
	] waqfa qadam: ∆ kunji2 ? ((kunji2 = 'ArrowLeft') ? -1 (kunji2 = 'ArrowRight') ? 1 ø) ø)
\

@Scene/step itni tadaad se hilta hai: @Phrase — \1\ aage, \-1\ peechhe — aur siron par aap ke liye ruk jata hai.
@Scene/go wohi khayaal hai, lekin faasle ki jagah ek maqaam ke taur par: us @Phrase ka number jo aap chahte hain, 1 se ginte hue, ya woh @Phrase/name jo aap ne usay diya.
