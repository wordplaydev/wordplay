Apne matn ko saja hua banayein

Kabhi kabhi hum chahte hain ke hamara matn alag nazar aaye. Hum yeh is tarah kar sakte hain: / tirchha karke /, * mota karke *, aur bhi bohat kuchh!

Misaal ke taur par, matn ko tirchha karne ke liye usay do slash // mein band karein, jaise //Hello//. Misaal:


\
Group(Tah() [
        Jumla(`Kya aap apne matn mein /tirchhe huroof/ istemaal karna chahte hain?`)
        Jumla(`Yeh kisi baat par zor dene ke liye ya apne alfaaz ko /khaas andaaz/ dene ke liye achha hai.`)
        Jumla(`Bas lafz ya jumle se pehle aur baad mein slash (//) lagayein, /is tarah/.`)
    ]
)
\


@Phrase matn ko mota bhi kar sakta hai. Iske liye apne matn ko do sitaron ** mein band karein, jaise **Hello**. Misaal:

\
Group(Tah() [
        Jumla(`Kya aap apne matn ko *mote huroof* se numaya karna chahte hain?`)
        Jumla(`Jab aapko *zor se pukarna* ho, tab yeh bilkul munasib hai.`)
        Jumla(`Bas apne alfaaz ke aas paas ek ek asterisk (**) lagayein, jaise *wah!*, aur farq dekhein.`)
      ]
)
\

@Phrase matn ko bohat hi mota bhi bana sakta hai. Iske liye apne matn ko do caret ^^ mein band karein, jaise ^^Hello^^. Misaal:

\
Group(Tah() [
        Jumla(`Jab aapko andaaz sab se zyada tez karna ho, tab ^bohat mote^ huroof sab se behtareen hain.`)
        Jumla(`Yeh mote huroof jaisa hai lekin zyada ^zordaar^, aur aam taur par ek ek caret (^^) lagane se banta hai.`)
        Jumla(`Isay ^sarkhiyon^ ya ^bari baaton^ ko numaya karne ke liye istemaal karein.`)
    ]
)
\

 Aakhir mein, @Phrase matn ke neeche lakeer bhi laga sakta hai. Iske liye apne matn ko do underscore __ mein band karein, jaise __Hello__. Misaal:

\
Group(Tah() [
        Jumla(`Jab aap matn ko saada rakhna chahte hain lekin phir bhi numaya karna chahte hain, tab _lakeer_ bohat achhi hai.`)
        Jumla(`Apne matn se pehle aur baad mein ek ek underscore (__) lagayein, _is tarah_, aur saaf zor mil jata hai.`)
        Jumla(`Yeh apni baat kehne ke liye _bilkul theek_ hai, bina _zarurat se zyada_ kiye.`)
    ]
)
\
