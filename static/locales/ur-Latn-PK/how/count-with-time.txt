Waqt guzarne ke saath ginti barhayein

Bohat si mansoobon mein aisi cheez chahiye hoti hai jo waqt ke saath barhti hai, jaise timer, score ya ulti ginti.
Hum ek counter bana sakte hain jab hum is dhaara ko jodein @Time dhaara ke saath ek @Reaction na ho.

Ek @Reaction ke teen hisse hote hain: ek aaghaazi qeemat, dekhne ke liye ek cheez, aur ek naya qeemat jab woh cheez badalti hai.
Yahan hum shuru karte hain \0\ se, dekhte hain @Time, aur har baar ghari tik karne par ek jama karte hain:

\
ginti: 0 … ∆ Time(1000ms) … ginti + 1
Phrase(ginti → '')
\

 Har second, @Time chalta hai, aur @Reaction isay dekh leta hai \∆ Time(1000ms)\, aur \count\ ban jata hai \count + 1\ na ho.
Yeh \→ ''\ aakhir mein number ko matn mein badal deta hai taake @Phrase isay dikha sake.

Aap koi bhi input istemaal kar sakte hain, sirf waqt hi nahin. Yeh counter Button() ke dabaaye jaane ki tadaad ginta hai:

\
ginti: 0 … ∆ Button() … ginti + 1
Phrase(ginti → '')
\

 Jab bhi @Button dhaara click ya tap se badalti hai, ginti ek se barh jati hai.
