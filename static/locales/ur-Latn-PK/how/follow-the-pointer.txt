Ek kirdaar ko apni ungli ke peeche chalaein

Kya aap chahte hain ke ek kirdaar aapke mouse ya ungli ka peecha kare, poore @Stage?
Hum yeh is ke saath kar sakte hain: @Pointer input.

@Pointer hamein har baar ek naya @Place deta hai jab pointer hilta hai.
Agar hum is @Place ko @Phrase ki \place\ bana dein, to kirdaar wahin jayega jahan hum ishaara karein.

\
Stage([Jumla('🐈' jagah: Nishaana())] jagah: Jagah(0m 0m -10m))
\

 Bas itna hi! Apna mouse hilaein ya ungli ghumaein, aur billi ko peeche aate dekhein.

Aap aur dilchasp cheezein kar sakte hain agar is mein se \x\ aur \y\ ko @Place na ho.
Yeh ek batakh hai jo hamesha pointer se ek meter upar tairta rehta hai:

\
Stage([Jumla('🦆' jagah: Jagah(Nishaana().x Nishaana().y + 1m))] jagah: Jagah(0m 0m -10m))
\
