Matn ko ek saath saath rakhein

Kabhi kabhi hum chahte hain ke matn ek doosre ke saath ek saaf lakeer mein baithe.
Hum yeh is se kar sakte hain: @Row, jo sab kuch ek @Group mein baayen se daayen tarteeb deta hai.

\
Group(Qataar() [
    Jumla('🍎')
    Jumla('🍌')
    Jumla('🍇')
])
\

 Ek @Row huroof ko unke oopar, beech, ya neeche ke kinaaron se bhi barabar kar sakta hai, aur unke darmiyaan jagah bhi daal sakta hai.
Yeh wahi phal hai, lekin neeche se barabar kiya gaya hai aur thodi khuli jagah ke saath:

\
Group(Qataar('>' 0.5m) [
    Jumla('🍎' size: 2m)
    Jumla('🍌' size: 1m)
    Jumla('🍇' size: 3m)
])
\

 Yeh \'>'\  @Row ko batata hai ke sab kuch apne neeche ke kinaare par barabar karo. Aazmaayein \'<'\ oopar ke liye ya \'|'\ beech ke liye.
Yeh \0.5m\ har harf ke darmiyaan ki jagah hai.
