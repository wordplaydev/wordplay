Kirdaar ko girne aur uchhalne wala banayein

Agar hum chahein ke koi kirdaar neeche gire, farsh se uchhle, ya asli cheez ki tarah dhakka khaye to kya karein?
Is ke liye yeh hain: @Motion aur @Matter — yeh mil kar ek @Phrase na ho.

@Motion ki physics chalate hain. Yeh tay karta hai ke kirdaar waqt ke saath / kahan / hai. Hum ise \place\ ke liye @Phrase ke tor par istemaal karte hain, aur ise unchaai se shuru karte hain taake yeh gire.
Hamein girne ke liye koi cheez bhi chahiye, warna hamara kirdaar hamesha girta hi rahega! Ek chauda @Shape wala @Rectangle achha farsh ban jata hai:

\
Stage([
    Jumla('🍎' jagah: Harkat(Jagah(0m 5m)) maadda: Maadda())
    Shakal(Mustateel(-10m 0m 10m -5m))
])
\

 Seb paanch meter unchaai se shuru hota hai aur gurutvakarshan se girta hai.
Jab yeh farsh se takrata hai to thoda uchhalta hai, kyunki @Phrase mein ek pehle se maujood @Matter hota hai jo tay karta hai ke yeh kitna bhaari aur uchhalne wala hai.

Kirdaar kitna / uchhalne wala / ya kitna / bhaari / hai, yeh badalne ke liye usay apna @Matter na ho.
@Matter dein. Yeh wazan, uchhaal aur ragad leta hai:

\
Stage([
    Jumla('⚽' jagah: Harkat(Jagah(-2m 5m)) maadda: Maadda(1kg 0.9 0.2))
    Jumla('🪨' jagah: Harkat(Jagah(2m 5m)) maadda: Maadda(5kg 0.1 0.8))
    Shakal(Mustateel(-10m 0m 10m -5m))
])
\

 Gend halki aur uchhalne wali hai, is liye woh wapas uchhal jati hai.
Patthar bhaari hai aur uchhalta nahin, is liye woh bas dham se gir jata hai.
