Manazir ki ek animated silsila banayein

Kabhi kabhi hum kahaniyan sunana, geet ke bol dikhana, ya shayari ko animate karna chahte hain.
Iske liye hum istemaal kar sakte hain @Scene aur @Phrase ko saath mein, taake ek tarteeb dikha sakein.

Misaal ke taur par, sochein ke hum chhoti si bachchon ki nazm "Jack be Nimble" ko animate karna chahte hain.
Hum ek @Scene bana sakte hain jis mein har misra ek @Phrase ho, aur har misre ki @Phrase/duration tay karke, @Scene aage barhega jab har lafzon ka tukda mukammal ho jaye.

\
Manzar([
  Jumla('Jack phurteela bano' muddat: 1s)
  Jumla('Jack tez bano' muddat: 1s)
  Jumla('Jack mombatti ke stand ke upar se koodo' muddat: 1s)
])
\

@Scene ek @Sequence ke mukammal hone ka bhi intezaar karega. Chalein, mombatti wale misre ko uchhalne dein!

\
Manzar([
		Jumla('Jack phurteela bano' muddat: 1s)
		Jumla('Jack tez bano' muddat: 1s)
		Jumla('Jack mombatti ke stand ke upar se koodo' aana: Silsila.uchhalna(muddat: 1s))
	])
\

@Scene ek @Group bhi le sakta hai matn ki, aur woh uske tamaam mawaad ki animation khatam hone ka intezaar karega.

\
Manzar([
		Group(Tah() [
      Jumla('Jack phurteela bano')
		  Jumla('Jack tez bano')
    ] muddat: 2s)
		Jumla('Jack mombatti ke stand ke upar se koodo' aana: Silsila.uchhalna(muddat: 1s))
	])
\

 Kabhi kabhi nazm ko chalte rehna chahiye. @Scene/loop  @Scene ko wapas ghuma kar apne pehle @Phrase par le aata hai jab aakhri khatam ho jaye, is liye woh khud ko baar baar dohrata hai.

\
Manzar([
		Jumla('Jack phurteela bano' muddat: 1s)
		Jumla('Jack tez bano' muddat: 1s)
		Jumla('Jack mombatti ke stand ke upar se koodo' muddat: 1s)
	] chakkar: ⊤)
\

 Yaad rakhein: ek @Scene tabhi wapas ghoomta hai jab list mein koi cheez waqai intezaar karti ho.
Woh list jis mein har @Phrase ki @Phrase/duration  \0s\ ho, computer ki poori raftaar se ghoomti rahegi, is liye woh aakhir mein ruk jati hai.
