Kisi harf ya shakal ki bohat si nakalein banayein

Agar hamein das billiyan ya sau sitare chahiye hon, lekin hum un sab ko ek ek kar ke likhna na chahein to?
Hum ek list se kah sakte hain ke woh hamare liye nakalein bana de, is ke zariye @List/translate na ho.

Agar hum is number se shuru karein \10\ aur use is se badal dein \→ []\, to hamein 1 se 10 tak das numbron ki ek list mil jaati hai.
Phir hum is ko istemaal karte hain @List/translate taake har number ko ek mein badal sakein @Phrase deta hai:

\
Group(Qataar() (10 → []).badlo(ƒ() Jumla('🐈')))
\

@List/translate hamare chhote se function ko har rukn ke liye ek baar chalata hai, aur nateejon ko ek nayi list mein jama kar deta hai.

Har nakal ko thoda alag banane ke liye, woh number istemaal karein jo translate hamein deta hai:

\
Group(Qataar('|' 0.2m) [:1‥8].badlo(ƒ(n4) Jumla('⭐' size: n4 × 0.5m)))
\

 Har sitara pichhle se bada hai, kyunke \n\ is se shuru hota hai \1\ aur is tak jaata hai \8\ na ho.
