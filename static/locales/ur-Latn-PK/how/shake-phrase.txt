Kirdaar ko hilaayein

Kabhi kabhi kirdaar ko kaanptay hue dikhana faaida mand hota hai.
Aisa karne ke kai tareeqay hain.

Hum istemaal kar sakte hain @Phrase/offset ke saath @Random aur @Time taake aisa ho jaye.

\
hataHua: 0m … ∆ Waqt() … Ittefaqi(-0.1m 0.1m)
Jumla('salaam' hatao: Jagah(hataHua 0m))
\

 Yeh ek qeemat banata hai jiska naam hai \offset\ jo har baar ghari ke chalne par ek naye random qeemat mein badal jati hai.
Phir yeh us offset ko istemaal karke dikhata hai @Phrase ko utni hi offset ke saath.

Agar hum hilne ko aur behtar tareeqay se qaaboo karna chahein, to hum istemaal kar sakte hain ek @Sequence taake ek random hilne wali animation banayein jo dohraati rahe.

\
Jumla('salaam' rukna: Silsila({
			0%: Andaaz(hatao: Jagah(Ittefaqi(-.2m .2m)))
			15%: Andaaz(hatao: Jagah(Ittefaqi(-.2m .2m)))
			60%: Andaaz(hatao: Jagah(Ittefaqi(-.2m .2m)))
			75%: Andaaz(hatao: Jagah(Ittefaqi(-.2m .2m)))
		} 0.1s))
\

 Yeh misaal sirf ek baar random qeematein chunti hai, lekin unhein ek dohraane wali tarteeb mein badal deti hai.
