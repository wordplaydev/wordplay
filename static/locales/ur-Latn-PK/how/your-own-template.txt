Apna khud ka character template banayein

Kabhi kabhi aap ko bohat saare character chahiye hote hain jo lagbhag ek jaise dikhte hain, lekin un mein chhote chhote farq hote hain.
Ek hi cheez ko baar baar likhne ke bajaye, aap @Phrase ke zariye apna template bana sakte hain. Template ek nuskhe ki tarah hai jis mein khaali jagahein bharni hoti hain. @Structure par ruk jaata hai.

Ek @Structure ek nuskhe ki tarah hai jis mein khaali jagahein bharni hoti hain.
Yeh ek ghubbare ka template hai jo rang aur label leta hai, aur apne aap ko dikhana jaanta hai:

\
•ghubbara(rang3•Rang parchi•'') (
    ƒ dikhayein() Jumla(`🎈 \parchi\` rang: rang3 size: 2m)
)

Group(Qataar() [
    ghubbara(Rang(80% 50 240°) 'Aasmaan').dikhayein()
    ghubbara(Rang(60% 80 0°) 'Gulaab').dikhayein()
    ghubbara(Rang(90% 80 90°) 'Dhoop').dikhayein()
])
\

 Yeh \•Balloon(...)\ hissa template ko tay karta hai, aur \ƒ dikhayein()\ hissa batata hai / ek ko kaise banana hai / par ruk jaata hai.
Phir hum alag alag rang aur label ke teen ghubbare banate hain, aur har ek se kehte hain ke woh khud ko \show\ kare.

Jab aap apna code saaf suthra aur apne character ek jaise rakhna chahein, tab templates bohat kaam aate hain.
