Music ko action ke saath chalayein

Jo game shuru se aakhir tak ek hi dhun bajaye, woh feeki lagti hai. Agar music ko pata ho ke aap jeet rahe hain ya nahin, to kya hoga?

Koi tareeqa nahin hai ke * rok * ek @Music ko — usay jo rokta hai woh yeh hai ke woh is par na ho: @Stage. Is liye ek haalat rakhein, aur us se milta hua gaana stage par rakhein:

\
marhala: 'menu' … ∆ Button() … (marhala = 'menu') ? 'peecha' 'menu'

mauzoo2: [1 ø 3 ø 5 ø 6 ø]

Stage([
	Jumla((marhala = 'menu') ? '▶ shuru karne ke liye dabayein' '🏃 daudein!' 1m)
	(marhala = 'menu')
		? Mauseeqi(Dhun(mauzoo2 saaz: Saaz.ghanti) raftaar: 72beats/min)
		Mauseeqi(Dhun(mauzoo2 saaz: Saaz.barqiBaaja) raftaar: 132beats/min)
])
\

 Dono gaane wohi aath numbers hain. Sirf saaz aur raftaar badalti hai, aur phir bhi yeh alag dhun lagti hai — kyunki degrees batate hain * scale ka kaun sa qadam *, koi sur nahin, is liye ek @Music unhein dobara samajh sakta hai. Usay yeh dein \paimaana2: Mauseeqi.hamaahangChhota\ aur wohi dhun dara dene wali ban jaati hai, ek bhi sur dobara likhe baghair.

Doosra hissa woh music hai jo halaat ke saath lagataar badalta hai. Badalne se \tempo\ kuch dobara shuru nahin hota — yeh agli dhun par jud jaata hai, is liye dhun tez ho jaati hai aur apni jagah nahin khoti:

\
score: 0 … ∆ Button() … score + 1
jaldi: (score ÷ 10).kamtareen(1)

Stage([
	Jumla('score \score\' 1m)
	Mauseeqi(
		Dhun([1 5 8 5] saaz: Saaz.dhol)
		raftaar: (90 + (jaldi · 90)) · 1beats/min
	)
])
\

 Kaafi baar dabayein to dhol shuru ke muqable mein doguni taaqat se bajte hain. Chhota aur tez sur is baat ko sunai deta hai — lambe ruke hue suron mein raftaar ka badalna mehsoos karna mushkil hota hai.
