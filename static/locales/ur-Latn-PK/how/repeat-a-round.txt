Khel ka ek daur dohraein

Bohat se khel wohi chand lamhon ko baar baar dohrate hain: batana ke kya karna hai, jawab ka intezaar karna, dikhana ke kaisa raha, phir dobara shuru karna.
Ek @Scene poora woh daur ban sakta hai, aur @Scene/loop use wapas shuru par le aata hai.

Yeh lamhe list ki cheezein hain, aur unke darmiyan ek @Boolean woh jagah hai jahan @Scene khiladi ka intezaar karta hai.

\
andaaza2: Guftagu()
daayein2: andaaza2 = 'cat'
Manzar([
	Jumla('Janwar ka andaaza lagayein!' muddat: 2s)
	∆ andaaza2
	Jumla(daayein2 ? 'Ji haan!' 'Nahin!' muddat: 2s)
] chakkar: ~daayein2)
\

 Yeh \∆ guess\ intezaar hai: yeh sirf usi lamhe sahi hota hai jab naya andaza aata hai, is liye @Scene wahin ruka rehta hai jab tak koi kuch type na kare.
Aur \chakkar2: ~right\ dohrana hai: @Scene baar baar wapas aata rehta hai jab tak woh sahi jawab na de dein, aur phir aakhri @Phrase par ruk jaata hai.

Note: @Scene/loop har baar parha jaata hai jab aapka program badalta hai, is liye aap chakkar ko khatam bhi kar sakte hain aur shuru bhi, jo abhi \~right\ ne kiya.

Daur kahan hai, yeh jaanne ke liye poochein @Spotlight par ruk jaata hai.
Yeh batata hai ke kaun sa @Phrase nazar aa raha hai, kitne hain, @Scene kitni baar wapas aaya hai, aur kya yeh abhi kisi ka intezaar kar raha hai.

\
andaaza2: Guftagu()
par2: Roshni()
Stage([
	Manzar([
		Jumla('Janwar ka andaaza lagayein!' muddat: 2s)
		∆ andaaza2
		Jumla('Nahin!' muddat: 2s)
	] chakkar: ⊤)
	Jumla(par2.intezaar ? 'apna andaaza likhein' `daur \par2.phera + 1\` size: 0.4m jagah: Jagah(0m -2m))
])
\

@Scene/replay daur ko jab chahein dobara shuru karne ka tareeqa hai, chahe woh kahin bhi pahunch chuka ho.
Ise koi aisi cheez dein jo sirf ek lamhe ke liye sahi ho, jaise \∆ Button()\ par ruk jaata hai.
