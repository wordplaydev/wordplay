Camera se video banayein

Yeh @Camera dhaara duniya se rangon ka jaal hasil karne ka bohat acha tareeqa hai. 
Agar hum un rangon ko dekhna chahein aur woh tasveer dobara banayein jo @Camera dekhta hai, to kya karein?

Ek @Image rangon ka jaal leta hai aur use ek tasveer ki shakal mein banata hai, is liye hum use @Camera ke rang seedhe de sakte hain.

\
chauraai: 24
unchaai2: 24
colors2: Camera(chauraai × 1px unchaai2 × 1px 33ms)
Stage([Tasveer(colors2 'camera jo dekhta hai' 12m)])
\

 Har @Image ko ek wazahat chahiye, kyunke rangon ke jaal ke paas apne koi alfaaz nahin hote jo us shakhs ke kaam aayein jo dekh nahin sakta.

Hum tasveer ko khaanon ki bajaye huroof se bana sakte hain, is ke liye use ek / glyph / dein, yaani ek function jo ek rang ko us matn mein badalta hai jo us rang ki numaindagi karta hai.

\
chauraai: 24
unchaai2: 24
colors2: Camera(chauraai × 1px unchaai2 × 1px 33ms)
Stage([Tasveer(colors2 'camera, jo gehre aur halke daairon se banaya gaya hai' 12m nishaan: ƒ(rang3•Rang) (rang3.ujaala < 50%) ? "⬤" "◯")])
\
