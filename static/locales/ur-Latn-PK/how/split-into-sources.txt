Mansooba ko maakhaz files mein taqseem karein

Ek mansooba mein ek se zyada @Source ho sakte hain. Data ki lambi list ko alag file mein rakhna
aapke main program ko chhota rakhta hai, taake woh ek nazar mein parha ja sake.

Farz karein aap ko chunne ke liye rangon ki list chahiye. Ek doosra @Source banayein, us ka naam 
/ colors / rakhein, aur list wahan likh dein. Naam se pehle \↑\ ka matlab hai / isay baant dein
mansooba ke baaqi hisson ke saath /:

\
↑ colors/en: ['🔴' '🟠' '🟡' '🟢' '🔵' '🟣']
\

 Yeh / en / naam ke baad batata hai ke naam kis zubaan mein likha hai, taake
doosri zubaan mein aap ka mansooba parhne wala jaan sake ke woh isay parh sakta hai ya nahin.

Ab apne main @Source mein wapas jaayein aur list ko @Borrow se andar layein. Likhein 
\↓\ aur phir doosre maakhaz ka naam:

\
↓ colors

Phrase(colors.random())
\🪲

 Is @Source, / colors / ke andar ab yeh us list ko batata hai jo aap ne baanti thi. Jis cheez par bhi aap
lagayein, \↑\ usay is tarah udhaar liya ja sakta hai: list, number, function, ya structure.

Naya @Source banane ke liye, / maakhaz shamil karein / button mansooba ke neeche hai.
