Apna banaya hua character dikhayein

Emoji, huroof aur nishaan bahut achhe hain! Lekin kabhi kabhi aap apne khud ke character banana chahte hain.
Aap apna character kaise banayein aur dikhayein?

Sab se pehle, is page par character banayein: < Kirdaar @://characters> page. 
Aap pixel, chaukor, gole aur raaste istemaal kar sakte hain. 
Usay ek naam dein, uske baare mein likhein, phir aap apna user naam aur character ka naam is tarah istemaal kar sakte hain:

\
Phrase(`Mera shandaar @username/charactername dekhein!`)
\

 Yeh user naam aur character ka naam maujood nahin hai, is liye yeh khaali chaukor ki tarah dikhega.
Apna asli user naam aur character ka naam hi istemaal karein

Dekhein, matn mein quote ke nishaan ki jagah backtick istemaal hua hai? Yeh zaroori hai. 
Yeh batata hai ke aap saja hua matn istemaal kar rahe hain, aur apne banaye hue character ko dikhane ke liye yeh zaroori hai.

Matn ko saja kar likhne ke baare mein aap mazeed seekh sakte hain: @Phrase na ho.
