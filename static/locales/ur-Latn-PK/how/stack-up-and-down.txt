Huroof ko upar se neeche jamaein

Agar @Row saath saath rakhne ke liye hai, to @Stack upar se neeche rakhne ke liye hai.
@Stack har harf ko pichhle harf ke neeche rakhta hai, ek meenaar ki tarah.

\
Group(Tah() [
    Jumla('ek')
    Jumla('do')
    Jumla('teen')
])
\

 Jaise @Row, @Stack mein bhi sath aur khaali jagah ho sakti hai.
Sab kuch baayein kinaare ke saath milane ki koshish karein:

\
Group(Tah('<' 0.25m) [
    Jumla('🐣 chhota')
    Jumla('🐥 darmiyana')
    Jumla('🐔 bara')
])
\

 Yeh \'<'\ ka matlab hai / sab kuch baayein kinaare ke saath milayein / na ho.
Aap \'|'\ darmiyan ke liye ya \'>'\ daayein kinaare ke liye bhi aazma sakte hain.
