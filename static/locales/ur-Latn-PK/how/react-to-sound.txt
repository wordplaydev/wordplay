Awaaz se kirdaaron ko bada karein

Kya aap jaante hain ke aapki mansooba aapki baat sun sakti hai? @Volume dhaara aapke microphone se ek number deti hai, jo \0\ (khaamosh) se \1\ (tez) tak hota hai.

Hum is number se kuch bhi qaabu kar sakte hain — jaise @Phrase ka size, uska ghoomna, uska rang.

\
Jumla('📣' size: (AwaazKiUnchaai() × 5m) + 1m)
\

 Jab khaamoshi ho, to megaphone \1m\ lamba hota hai. Jab aap cheekhte hain, to woh \6m\ tak lamba ho sakta hai.

Kya aap ek aisa sooraj chahte hain jo aapki awaaz jitni tez ho, utna tez ghoome?

\
Jumla('🌞' ghumaao: AwaazKiUnchaai() × 360°)
\

 Taali bajaein ya gunguna kar dekhein, aur dekhein ke kya hota hai.
(Pehli baar aapka computer microphone istemaal karne ki ijaazat maang sakta hai.)
