Apne dekhne walon ko saaz chunne dein

Iske baare mein sab kuch @Music ke bajne ke dauran tay kiya ja sakta hai, yahan tak ke yeh bhi ke use kaun bajaye. Dosre @Track ko ek alag @Instrument dein aur dhun apni jagah qaayam rehti hai — sirf aawaaz badalti hai.

@Choice us muntakhab hone wale output ki \name\ wapas deta hai jo dekhne walon ne chuna, is liye ek shart hi kaafi hai ke use saaz bana diya jaye:

\
chunaGaya: Intikhaab()

Stage([
	Group(Qataar() [
		Jumla('🎹' chunaJaSakneWala naam: 'piano')
		Jumla('🎻' chunaJaSakneWala naam: 'violin')
	])
	Mauseeqi(
		Dhun(
			[1 3 5 6 5 3]
			saaz: (chunaGaya = 'violin') ? Saaz.violin Saaz.piano
		)
	)
])
\

 Yeh do muntakhab hone wale phrase hi is ko kaam karne ke qaabil banate hain — @Choice ke paas parhne ko kuch nahin hota jab tak stage par koi cheez \chunneKeQabil: ⊤\ na ho.

Do saaz ek shart mein aa jaate hain. Poori palette nahin aati, aur aap list do baar likhne par majboor ho jaate hain: ek baar buttons ke liye aur ek baar aawaaz ke liye. Is ke bajaye unhein apni banai hui ek structure mein ek saath rakhein, aur dono usi se banayein:

\
•aawaaz2(nishaan2•'' aawaaz3•Saaz)

aawaazein•[aawaaz2]: [
	aawaaz2('🎹' Saaz.piano)
	aawaaz2('🎺' Saaz.bigul)
	aawaaz2('🔔' Saaz.ghanti)
]

chunaGaya: Intikhaab()
milGaya: aawaazein.dhoondo(ƒ(aawaaz4•aawaaz2) aawaaz4.nishaan2 = chunaGaya)
maujooda•aawaaz2: milGaya•ø ? aawaazein[1] milGaya

Stage([
	Group(Qataar() [
		:aawaazein.badlo(
			ƒ(aawaaz5•aawaaz2) Jumla(aawaaz5.nishaan2 chunaJaSakneWala naam: aawaaz5.nishaan2)
		)
	])
	Mauseeqi(Dhun([1 3 5 6 5 3] saaz: maujooda.aawaaz3))
])
\

 Chauthi aawaaz shamil karna ab sirf ek line hai, aur button khud ba khud nazar aa jaata hai. Is \found•ø ?\ nigehbaan par ghaur karein: @List/find jab kuch bhi mel na khaye to \ø\ wapas deta hai, aur aisa tab hota hai jab tak kisi ne kuch chuna na ho — aur badal poora \Bolna\ hona chahiye, kyunki \current\ yehi wada karta hai.
