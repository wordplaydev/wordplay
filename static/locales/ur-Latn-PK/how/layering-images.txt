Tasveeron ko ek doosre ke oopar rakhein

Kabhi kabhi hum tasveeron ko ek ke oopar ek rakhna chahte hain taake mazedaar manzar ya takhleeqi shaklein banayein.

Misaal ke taur par, aap apni mansooba mein kai tahein istemaal kar sakte hain @Stage par, taake tasveeron ko jaise chahein tarteeb se rakhein.

Dekhein! Yeh kachhua maine sirf shaklon se banaya hai!
Har shakl ke number badal kar dekhein. Aap unhein hila sakte hain, bada chhota kar sakte hain, aur ghuma bhi sakte hain.
Khel kar dekhein ke aap kya kuch bana sakte hain!

\
¶Note: Tasveerein ek ke upar ek lagane ki aik aur mansooba <misaal@https://wordplay.dev/project/example-Layers?play> ke liye numaish gah dekhein.¶
