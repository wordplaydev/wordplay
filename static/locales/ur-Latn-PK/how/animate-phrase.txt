Kirdaar ko harkat dein

Kirdaar ki ek aaraam ki animation ho sakti hai jo us waqt dohraati rehti hai jab woh hil nahin rahe hote.

Misaal ke taur par, aayein is billi ko ghoomne aur khisakne se thoda sa nachaain:

\
Phrase(
    '🐈' 
    rukna: Sequence({
        0%: Pose(ghumaao: 0°)
        15%: Pose(ghumaao: 15°)
        30%: Pose(ghumaao: 0°)
        45%: Pose(hatao: Place(-0.25m))
        60%: Pose(hatao: Place(0.25m))
        75%: Pose(hatao: Place(-0.25m))
        90%: Pose(hatao: Place(0m))
    } 2s)
)
\

 Is misaal mein ek istemaal hota hai @Sequence alag alag ka @Pose.
