# Emails

Every email Wordplay sends. Generated by `npm run emails` from
[functions/src/email/registry.ts](functions/src/email/registry.ts) — edit that
rather than this file. `emailSheet.test.ts` fails when the two drift.

All of it is sent from `Wordplay <hi@mail.wordplay.dev>`, replying to
`hi@wordplay.dev`: a verified subdomain, so the reputation of
Wordplay's mail is independent of the Workspace mail sent from wordplay.dev.

Run `npm run emails` and open `build/emails/index.html` to see each one
rendered, in both light and dark.

| Email                    | What it is                                                      | Subject (English)                                    | Sent when                                                  | To                                       | Translated | Optional                                             |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| `signin`                 | The link that signs someone in, or finishes joining.            | Your Wordplay sign-in link                           | the sendSigninLink or joinAccount callable                 | the person signing in                    | yes        | no — it was asked for seconds earlier                |
| `feedback`               | What a creator said about Wordplay, sent to whoever reads hi@.  | New feedback: The stage goes blank when I press play | a document created in feedback/{id}                        | hi@wordplay.dev                          | no         | no — it goes to us, not to a creator                 |
| `notice-reported`        | Something you made was reported and is being reviewed.          | Your project was reported                            | the report callable, through deliver()                     | whoever made it                          | yes        | yes — "decisions about you", on the profile page     |
| `notice-decision`        | A decision about something you made, with the moderator’s note. | A decision about your project                        | the moderate callable, through deliver()                   | whoever made it                          | yes        | yes — "decisions about you", on the profile page     |
| `notice-howto-published` | A how-to was published in a gallery you are in.                 | A new how-to in your gallery                         | the howToEdited trigger, when published first becomes true | the gallery’s curators and creators      | yes        | yes — "messages and new posts", off unless chosen    |
| `review-digest`          | How much is waiting in a reviewer’s queue.                      | 3 things are waiting for you to review               | a daily scheduled sweep                                    | platform moderators and gallery curators | yes        | yes — "waiting for your review", on the profile page |
| `chat-digest`            | How many conversations have something unread in them.           | You have unread chats                                | a scheduled sweep every few hours                          | anyone who asked for it                  | yes        | yes — "messages and new posts", off unless chosen    |
