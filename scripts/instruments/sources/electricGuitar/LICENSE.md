# Electro acoustique Sherwood SH887

A Sherwood SH887 electro-acoustic guitar, plucked, one note per file.

Downloaded from the Freesound pack [Electro acoustique Sherwood SH887](https://freesound.org/people/Project16/packs/15407/) by
**Project16** and used under [CC-BY-3.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), which asks
for credit and permits the derivatives we ship.

Only the files below are kept — one mid-velocity layer at roughly every
fifth semitone, which is as close together as the player needs zones to sit.
The rest of the pack, including its fret-noise effects and its other velocity
layers, is not in this repository. Each file here is the untouched original;
the build trims, normalizes, and re-encodes it into
`static/instruments/electricGuitar/`, and `static/instruments/CREDITS.md` carries
the same credit beside the shipped audio.
